Frequently Asked Questions

What is this app used for?

SmartSprout is an intelligent irrigation management system that helps farmers and landscapers optimize water usage through automated scheduling, real-time monitoring, and data-driven insights.

Do I need special equipment?

Yes, you'll need compatible irrigation controllers and sensors that can connect to our system. We provide a list of supported devices and can help you set up the necessary hardware.

Can I control the system from my phone?

Absolutely! Our mobile app allows you to monitor and control your irrigation system from anywhere. You can adjust schedules, check status, and receive notifications on your smartphone or tablet.

How accurate is the weather-based scheduling?

Our system uses multiple weather data sources and local sensors to provide highly accurate scheduling. The system continuously learns and adapts to your specific location and conditions.

What kind of support do you provide?

We offer 24/7 customer support, comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, and personalized setup assistance to ensure you get the most out of your SmartSprout system.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we use enterprise-grade security measures to protect your data. All information is encrypted, and we never share your data with third parties without your explicit consent.
